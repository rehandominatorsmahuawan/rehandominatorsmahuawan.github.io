Rehan Dominators Mahuawan (RDM) Cricket Team

Rehan Dominators Mahuawan, also known as RDM or RDM Mahuawan, is a cricket team from Mahuawan, Gopalganj, Bihar. This is the official RDM website for the team squad, players, matches, records, tournaments, honours, news, gallery and updates.

Official squad: Rehan Akhtar, Saif Ali, Soaib Akhtar, Akib Ansari, Asif Ali, Wasim, Jubed, Gulam, Sahil Ansari, Sahil Ali, Lucky, Kaif Lefty, SK. Hanif, Abushad, Sabre Alam, Zishan and Javed.
