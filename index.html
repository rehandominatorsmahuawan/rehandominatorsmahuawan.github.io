Club Heritage

Rehan Dominators Mahuawan (RDM) Cricket Team

Founded in 2024 in Mahuawan, Gopalganj, Bihar, Rehan Dominators Mahuawan (RDM) is a competitive village and regional cricket club built around discipline, brotherhood, and fearless limited-overs cricket. Champions of Mahuawan Premier League Seasons 1 & 2, RDM competes across Gopalganj district tournaments.

Home GroundMahuawan, Gopalganj
Club ColoursMidnight Cobalt & Stadium Gold
FormatsLimited Overs & T20
CaptaincyRehan Akhtar & Saif Ali

Official Squad: Rehan Akhtar, Saif Ali, Soaib Akhtar, Akib Ansari, Asif Ali, Wasim, Jubed, Gulam, Sahil Ansari, Sahil Ali, Lucky, Kaif Lefty, SK. Hanif, Abushad, Sabre Alam, Zishan, and Javed.
